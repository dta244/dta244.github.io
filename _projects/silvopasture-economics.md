## The decision

Silvopasture integrates trees into grazing land. It promises higher long-run returns and meaningful carbon
storage, but conversion is expensive up front and the payoff arrives over decades. Landowners, extension
agents, and conservation programs all need a defensible answer to a narrow question: under what conditions
does conversion pencil out?

## Approach

Enterprise budgets for conventional pasture and silvopasture systems are combined with biophysical estimates
of carbon accumulation, then carried through a discounted cash flow over a full rotation. The comparison
holds site quality and management intensity fixed so the difference reflects the system, not the land.

## What this supports

Results speak to the size and timing of carbon payment needed to close the gap for a representative operation.
They do not speak to adoption behaviour, credit market access, or heterogeneity across soil types, all of which
would need separate work.
